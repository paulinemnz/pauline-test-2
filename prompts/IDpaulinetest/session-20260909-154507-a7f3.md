## Summary

The [case brief](</C:/Users/pauli/OneDrive/Documents/ChatGPT/water under pressure test/WATER_Case_Brief.md>) reveals four central challenges:

- Water hazard data exists, but it is fragmented and rarely translated into operational or financial decisions.
- Water is inexpensive, so savings alone rarely justify investment; avoided downtime, compliance, and production capacity are the stronger business cases.
- Facility-level operational data is generally private, making exposure estimates dependent on transparent assumptions.
- Current drought conditions and long-term structural scarcity are different risks and must not be conflated.

Important case facts include seasonal scarcity across roughly 30% of EU territory, about one-third of public-supply water lost through leakage, a 52% rise in agricultural groundwater abstraction since 2000, and potential industrial downtime costs of up to €1 million per hour.

The selected opportunity is a map-based cockpit for an insurer or lender screening real French chemical-industry facilities. It will answer: **Which facilities merit deeper water-risk diligence, why, and what could disruption cost under explicit user assumptions?**
